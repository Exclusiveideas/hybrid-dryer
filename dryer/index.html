Autonomous hybrid solar dryer

A solar dryer for tomatoes and peppers that stores heat in wax for the night, dries evenly across three air zones, senses moisture live, and lets AI decide how to use sun, stored heat and backup heat. Sorting, vision grading and smart storage complete the line.

All-in-one machine: roof collector, reflector wing, wax heat wall, chamber, sorting bay, storage drawers and electronics in one wheeled body.
