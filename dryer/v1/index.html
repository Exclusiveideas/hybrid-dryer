Version 1: interactive 3D prototype

A shared picture of the whole machine before any metal is cut, so all nine team members agree on layout, sizes and how their parts connect.

Open the 3D prototype

What this version set out to do

  • Show the complete system in 3D: collector, trough boost, wax heat store, three-zone chamber, agitator, sensors, control, AI unit, sorting conveyor, vision station and smart storage.
  • Explain every part on tap: owner, what it does, key components, build tip and what to measure.
  • Let the team swap options and compare: collector size, concentrator on or off, wax amount, one or three zones, agitator, thermostat or AI, backup heater, weather, batch size.
  • Estimate drying time, heat after sunset, backup energy, tray evenness, solar share and rough build cost in naira.
  • Animate a day and a full drying batch: sun tracking, wax melting, airflow, product darkening zone by zone.
  • Work on phones and projectors for supervisor meetings.
