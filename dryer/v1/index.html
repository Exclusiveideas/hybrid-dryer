What it does, in one line

It dries sliced tomatoes and peppers with sunlight, keeps drying at night with heat stored in wax, and runs itself using sensors and AI.

How it works

  1. SortA conveyor carries fresh produce under a camera. Bad pieces are pushed off; good ones are sliced and loaded on trays.
  2. Heat the airA flat solar panel on legs warms outside air. A small curved mirror (the trough) follows the sun and adds extra heat on clear days.
  3. Store heatThe warm air passes a box of wax tubes. The wax melts by day and gives heat back after sunset.
  4. DryHot air goes into a cabinet with three zones of trays. Each zone has its own fan, and a rake turns the pieces so they dry evenly.
  5. ThinkSensors weigh the trays and read the air. The controller and AI decide when to use sun, wax heat or the backup heater, and when the batch is done.
  6. StoreDried product goes into sealed drums that watch humidity and warn before mould starts.

Using the 3D model

  • Tap a part or pick it under “Go to part” to zoom in and read about it.
  • On a selected part, use “Explode this part” to see its pieces, and “Isolate” to fade everything else.
  • Change options on the left; the numbers on the right update. “Run a batch” shows the trays drying.
