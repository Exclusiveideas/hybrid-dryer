Projects / Hybrid solar dryer / Version 2

Version 2: one all-in-one machine

Everything from version 1, packed into a single cabinet on wheels: 3.1 m long, 1.1 m deep, 2.5 m tall.

Open the 3D prototype

What it does, in one line

Put fresh produce in the hopper on the left; take dried, graded product out of the drawers on the right. The sun does most of the heating, even at night.

How it works

  1. LoadFresh produce goes into the hopper on the left. A short belt carries it through a dark hood with a camera and light. Bad pieces drop into a reject drawer; good ones reach the loading shelf.
  2. Heat the airThe whole roof is a solar panel that warms air. A mirror lid at the back opens on sunny days to bounce in extra sunlight, and closes over the glass at night or in storms.
  3. Store heatThe hot air flows down through a wall of wax tubes behind the trays. The wax melts by day and keeps the air warm after sunset.
  4. DryFans in that wall push hot air across three zones of trays behind glass doors. Rakes turn the pieces. Moist air leaves through the chimney.
  5. ThinkSensors weigh the trays and read the air. The controller in the base and the AI decide the heat source, fan speeds and when to open the mirror. A touchscreen shows everything.
  6. StoreDried product goes into sealed drawers on the right that watch humidity and CO₂ and warn before mould starts.

Using the 3D model

  • Pick a part under “Go to part” to zoom in; everything else fades so you can see it even inside the cabinet.

  • Change options on the left; the numbers on the right update. “Run a batch” shows the trays drying.
