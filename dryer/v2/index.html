Projects / Hybrid solar dryer / Version 2

Version 2: one all-in-one machine

Version 1 showed separate stations joined by ducts and pipes. Version 2 packs every function into one wheeled body, so heat travels shorter paths, the machine is easier to move and present, and it reads as a single product.

Open the 3D prototype

What this version set out to do

  • Merge all modules into one frame: processing bay on the left, three-zone chamber in the middle, storage drawers on the right, electronics in the base.
  • Make the roof the solar collector, with a PV strip over the processing bay for the electronics.
  • Replace the separate tracked trough with a mirrored reflector wing hinged on the roof. It opens to boost the collector and closes over the glass at night and in bad weather.
  • Move the wax store inside the machine as a heat wall behind the chamber, so hot air passes through it with no external ducts.
  • Add a cutaway view to look inside the closed body, plus exploded view to pull modules apart.
  • Add a touchscreen and emergency stop on the front pillar, and casters for moving the machine.

Known limits
